Rule 21: Definitions

Masthead Light

White light placed over the fore and aft centerline. Unbroken arc of 225° (from dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam on both sides).

Sidelights

Green (Starboard) and Red (Port). Each shows an unbroken arc of 112.5° (from dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam on their respective side).

Sternlight

White light placed as nearly as practicable at the stern. Unbroken arc of 135° (67.5° from right aft on each side).

Towing Light

Yellow light having the same characteristics as the sternlight (135° arc aft).

All-Round Light

A light showing an unbroken arc of 360°.

Flashing Light

A light flashing at regular intervals at a frequency of 120 flashes or more per minute.

Rule 23: Power-Driven Vessels Underway

Under 7 Meters & < 7 Knots

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • May exhibit an all-round white light.
  • Must also exhibit sidelights if practicable.

Under 12 Meters

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • May exhibit an all-round white light and sidelights.
  • (This acts in lieu of a separate masthead + sternlight).

Under 50 Meters

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • One Masthead light (White, Fwd).
  • Sidelights (Red/Green).
  • Sternlight (White).

50 Meters or Greater

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • Two Masthead lights (Fwd is lower, Aft is higher).
  • Sidelights (Red/Green).
  • Sternlight (White).

Air-Cushion Vessel (Hovercraft)

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • Must display standard power-driven vessel lights.
  • PLUS: An all-round flashing yellow light (when operating in non-displacement mode).

Rule 24: Towing & Pushing

Towing Astern (Tow < 200m)

Flip for details
NIGHT (TUG & TOW)
  • Tug: 2 Masthead lights. Sidelights. Towing Light (Yellow) OVER Sternlight (White).
  • Towed Object: Sidelights and a Sternlight.
  • No day shape required for <200m tow.

Towing Astern (Tow > 200m)

Flip for details
NIGHT
DAY
  • Tug: 3 Masthead lights. Yellow over White stern. Sidelights.
  • Towed Object: Sidelights, Sternlight.
  • Day Shape: Diamond shape is required on BOTH the tug and the tow if the tow length exceeds 200m.

Pushing Ahead (Inland)

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • Tug: 2 Mastheads. Sidelights. Two Towing Lights (Yellow OVER Yellow) at stern.
  • Towed Object: Sidelights at forward end. Special flashing yellow (Inland only).
  • Rigidly Connected Note: If tug and tow are rigidly connected in a composite unit, they are treated as a single power-driven vessel and show standard power vessel lights instead of towing lights.

Inconspicuous / Submerged Tow

Flip for details
  • Rule 24(g). Applies to logs, dracones, or other partially submerged objects being towed.
  • Night: All-round white lights at or near the forward and after ends (except dracones need no fwd light). If tow is >100m long, extra white lights so distance between is <100m. If >25m wide, white lights at lateral extremities.
  • Day: Diamond shape at or near after end. If tow is >200m long, an additional diamond shape at fwd end.

Rule 25: Sailing Vessels & Oars

Sailing Vessel (Standard)

Flip for details
NIGHT
  • Must display Sidelights & Sternlight ONLY (No white masthead light).
  • Optional addition: Two all-round lights at top of mast, Red over Green ("Sailing Machine").

Sailing Exceptions (<20m & <7m)

Flip for details
DAY (MOTORSAILING)
  • Under 20 meters: Sidelights and sternlight may be combined into one tri-color lantern at top of the mast. (If using tri-color, you CANNOT use the optional red-over-green mast lights).
  • Under 7m / Under Oars: Should exhibit normal lights if practicable. If not, must have an electric torch or lighted lantern showing a white light ready in sufficient time to prevent collision.
  • Motorsailing (Day): A vessel proceeding under sail AND machinery must exhibit a cone apex pointing down forward.

Rule 26: Fishing Vessels

Engaged in Fishing (Lines/Nets)

Flip for details
NIGHT (MAKING WAY)
DAY
  • All-round Red over White.
  • Sidelights and sternlight are added only when making way through the water.
  • Day Shape: Hourglass (2 cones apex to apex).

Engaged in Trawling

Flip for details
NIGHT (MAKING WAY)
DAY
  • All-round Green over White.
  • Optional: Masthead light abaft of and higher than the all-round green (Required if >50m).
  • Day Shape: Hourglass. Sidelights/Sternlight if making way.

Rule 27: NUC & RAM

Not Under Command (NUC)

Flip for details
NIGHT (MAKING WAY)
DAY
  • Red over Red all-round lights. No masthead light.
  • Sidelights & Sternlight added ONLY if making way.
  • Day shape: Two black balls.

Restricted Maneuverability (RAM)

Flip for details
NIGHT (MAKING WAY)
DAY
  • Red-White-Red all-round vertical lights.
  • Standard power lights (Masthead, sidelights, sternlight) added when making way.
  • Day Shape: Ball-Diamond-Ball.

RAM: Towing Unable to Deviate

Flip for details
  • Rule 27(c). A vessel engaged in a towing operation such as severely restricts the towing vessel and her tow in their ability to deviate from their course.
  • Must display normal towing lights (Rule 24).
  • PLUS: The RAM lights (Red-White-Red) and shapes (Ball-Diamond-Ball).

RAM: Dredging/Underwater Ops

Flip for details
  • Rule 27(d). Must exhibit the standard RAM lights/shapes.
  • PLUS (Safe Side): Two Green lights (or two diamonds) on the side it is safe to pass.
  • PLUS (Obstructed Side): Two Red lights (or two balls) on the side the obstruction exists.

RAM: Small Diving Operations

Flip for details
  • Rule 27(e). Whenever the size of a vessel engaged in diving operations makes it impracticable to exhibit all lights/shapes of 27(d).
  • Must exhibit Red-White-Red all-round lights.
  • Day Shape: A rigid replica of the International Code flag "A" (Alpha) not less than 1 meter in height.

RAM: Mineclearance Ops

Flip for details
  • Rule 27(f). Must exhibit standard power-driven vessel lights.
  • PLUS: Three all-round Green lights (or three black balls). One at or near foremast head, one at each end of the fore yard (looks like a cross).
  • Indicates it is dangerous for another vessel to approach within 1000 meters.

Rule 28: Constrained By Draft (CBD)

CBD (International Only)

Flip for details
NIGHT
DAY
  • In addition to the regular lights for a power-driven vessel of its size, may also display:
  • Three all-round Red lights in a vertical line.
  • Day Shape: One cylinder.

Rule 29: Pilot Vessels

Engaged on Pilotage Duty

Flip for details
NIGHT (UNDERWAY)
  • At or near the masthead, two all-round lights: White over Red.
  • When underway, in addition, sidelights and a sternlight.
  • When at anchor, in addition to the White over Red, the normal anchor light/shape.

Rule 30: Anchored & Aground

Vessels at Anchor

Flip for details
NIGHT (<50m)
DAY
  • < 50m: One all-round white light where best seen.
  • ≥ 50m: Two all-round white lights (fwd is higher).
  • Day Shape: One black ball forward.

Vessels Aground

Flip for details
NIGHT
DAY
  • Must exhibit the anchor lights prescribed in Rule 30(a).
  • PLUS: Two all-round red lights in a vertical line.
  • Day shape: Three black balls in a vertical line.

Rule 31: Seaplanes

Seaplanes & WIG Craft

Flip for details
  • Where it is impracticable for a seaplane or a WIG (Wing-In-Ground) craft to exhibit lights and shapes of the characteristics or in the exact positions described, she shall exhibit lights and shapes as closely similar in characteristics and position as is possible.
  • A WIG craft only when taking off, landing, or in flight near the surface shall, in addition to standard lights, exhibit a high-intensity all-round flashing red light.
