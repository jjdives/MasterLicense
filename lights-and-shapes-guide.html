Rule 21: Definitions

Light Arcs & Definitions

Click to flip

Light Arcs & Definitions

  • Masthead Light: White light over fore/aft centerline. Shows unbroken arc of 225° (from dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam on both sides).
  • Sidelights: Green (Starboard) and Red (Port). Each shows an unbroken arc of 112.5° (from dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam on its respective side).
  • Sternlight: White light placed as nearly as practicable at the stern. Shows unbroken arc of 135° (67.5° from right aft on each side).
  • Towing Light: Yellow light having the same characteristics as the sternlight (135°).
  • All-Round Light: A light showing an unbroken arc of 360°.
  • Flashing Light: Flashes at regular intervals at a frequency of 120 flashes or more per minute.

Rule 24: Towing & Pushing

Towing Astern

Click to flip

Towing Astern

Towing Vessel Displays:

  • Standard Sidelights.
  • Stern: Yellow OVER White ("Tow line is tight").
  • Mast: 2 Whites if tow < 200m. 3 Whites if tow > 200m.
  • Day Shape: Diamond only if tow exceeds 200m.

Towed Object Displays:

  • Sidelights and a Sternlight.
  • Day Shape: Diamond only if tow exceeds 200m.

Pushing Ahead & Rigid Composite

Click to flip

Pushing Ahead & Rigid Composite

  • Rigidly Connected: If pushing vessel and pushed vessel are rigidly connected in a composite unit, they are regarded as one power-driven vessel and show standard lights.
  • Pushing Ahead (Inland): Towing vessel shows Sidelights, 2 Masthead lights, and Yellow OVER Yellow at the stern. Pushed vessel shows Sidelights at forward end.

Submerged Objects (Logs/Dracones)

Click to flip

Submerged Objects

  • Rule 24(g). An inconspicuous, partly submerged vessel or object being towed shall exhibit:
  • One all-round white light at or near the forward end.
  • One all-round white light at or near the after end.
  • Day Shape: Diamond shape at or near the aftermost extremity.

Rule 25: Sailing Vessels Underway & Oars

Sailing Vessels

Click to flip

Sailing Vessels

  • Base Requirement: Sidelights and a Sternlight. (No white masthead light).
  • If < 20 meters: Sidelights and sternlight may be combined in one tricolor lantern at the top of the mast.
  • Optional Lights: May display all-round Red over Green at top of mast ("Sailing Machine"). Note: Cannot be used if using a tricolor lantern.
  • Motorsailing: If propelled by machinery, must act as a power-driven vessel. Day shape: Cone apex pointing down.

Sail < 7m & Vessels Under Oars

Sail < 7m & Vessels Under Oars

  • Shall, if practicable, exhibit normal sailing lights.
  • If not, they shall have ready at hand an electric torch or lighted lantern showing a white light.
  • This light shall be exhibited in sufficient time to prevent collision.

RAM: Diving (Small Vessels)

  • If size makes standard RAM lights/shapes impracticable:
  • Must display Red-White-Red all-round lights.
  • Day Shape: A rigid replica of the International Code flag "A" (Alpha) at least 1m high.

Mineclearance Vessels

Click to flip

Mineclearance Vessels

  • In addition to standard power-driven vessel lights.
  • Three all-round Green lights (one at foremast head, one at each end of foreyard).
  • Day Shape: Three black balls in the same configuration. Indicates it is dangerous to approach within 1000m.

  • In addition to the regular lights a vessel of that size must display (power-driven lights), also display:

Rule 29: Pilot Vessels

Click to flip

Pilot Vessels

  • White over Red ("Pilot Ahead" or "White hat, red nose").
  • Sidelights and sternlight apply when underway.
  • When at anchor, shows White over Red PLUS normal anchor lights.

Rule 30: Anchored & Aground

Click to flip

Anchored & Aground

Anchored:

  • Under 50m: One all-round white light.
  • Over 50m: Two all-round white lights (fwd is higher).
  • Day Shape: One Black Ball.

Aground:

  • Anchor lights PLUS Two vertical Red lights.
  • Day Shape: Three Black Balls.

Rule 31: Seaplanes

Click to flip

Seaplanes & WIG Craft

  • Where it is impracticable for a seaplane or a WIG (Wing-In-Ground) craft to exhibit lights and shapes of the characteristics or in the exact positions specified in the Rules...
  • She shall exhibit lights and shapes as closely similar in characteristics and position as is possible.
