Rule 21: Definitions

These definitions apply universally to all lights discussed below.

  • Masthead Light: White light over centerline. Unbroken arc of 225° (dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam on both sides).
  • Sidelights: Green (Stbd) and Red (Port). Arc of 112.5° each (dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam).
  • Sternlight: White light placed as nearly as practicable at the stern. Arc of 135°.
  • Towing Light: Yellow light having the same characteristics as the sternlight (135° arc).
  • All-Round Light: A light showing an unbroken arc of 360°.
  • Flashing Light: Flashes at regular intervals at a frequency of 120 flashes or more per minute.
