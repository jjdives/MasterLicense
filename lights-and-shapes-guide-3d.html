Rule 21: Definitions

Light Arcs & Definitions

Click to flip

Light Arcs & Definitions

  • Masthead Light: White light over centerline. Unbroken arc of 225° (dead ahead to 22.5° abaft the beam).
  • Sidelights: Green (Stbd) and Red (Port). Arc of 112.5° each.
  • Sternlight: White light at the stern. Arc of 135°.
  • Towing Light: Yellow light having the same characteristics as the sternlight (135°).
  • All-Round Light: Unbroken arc of 360°.
  • Flashing Light: Flashes 120 or more times per minute.

Sailing (< 7m) & Rowboats

Pilot & Anchored
