Lights & Shapes Visual Guide

A pedagogical guide showing exact physical placement on the vessel.

Diagram Key: In all diagrams, the Bow points to the Right. Top Views show the Port side (Red) at the top edge, and Starboard side (Green) at the bottom edge. Side Views look directly at the Starboard side.

1. Power-Driven Vessels (Underway)

Under 50 Meters

  • 1 Masthead (White, Fwd)
  • Sidelights (Red/Green)
  • Sternlight (White)
NIGHT (SIDE)
NIGHT (TOP)

Over 50 Meters

  • 2 Mastheads (Aft is higher)
  • Sidelights & Sternlight
NIGHT (SIDE)
NIGHT (TOP)

2. Sailing Vessels

  • Sidelights & Sternlight ONLY (No white masthead light).
  • Optional at top of mast: Red over Green ("Sailing Machine").
  • If using a motor, they display a cone apex down (acting as a power vessel).
NIGHT (SIDE)
DAY (MOTORSAILING)

3. Fishing & Trawling

Fishing (Lines/Nets)

  • Red over White ("Frying Fish Tonight").
  • Sidelights/Sternlight added ONLY if making way.
NIGHT (SIDE)
DAY (SIDE)

Trawling (Dragging net)

  • Green over White ("Trawling Tonight").
  • Optional aft masthead if >50m.
NIGHT (SIDE)
DAY (SIDE)

4. Not Under Command (NUC)

  • Red over Red ("Captain is dead").
  • No masthead light.

DAY

5. Restricted Maneuver (RAM)

  • Red-White-Red ("Working ahead").
  • Normal power lights added if making way.
NIGHT
DAY

6. Constrained by Draft (CBD - Int'l Only)

  • 3 Reds in a row ("No room below").
  • Day shape is a single cylinder.

DAY

7. Towing & Pushing

Towing Astern

  • Yellow OVER White at the stern.
  • Mast: 2 Whites if tow < 200m, 3 Whites if > 200m.
NIGHT (SIDE)
DAY (>200M TOW)

Pushing Ahead (Inland)

  • Yellow OVER Yellow at the stern.
  • Mast: 2 Whites.
NIGHT (SIDE)

8. Anchored & Aground

Anchored

  • One all-around white light (fwd).
  • Shape: One black ball.

DAY

Aground

  • Anchor light(s) + Red over Red.
  • Shape: Three black balls ("Not going anywhere at all").
