The Puget Sound Exception

Despite being entirely enclosed by land, Puget Sound is not governed by Inland Rules.

Under 33 CFR § 80.1395, the 72 COLREGS (International Rules) apply to all waters of the Strait of Juan de Fuca, Haro Strait, Strait of Georgia, Puget Sound, Hood Canal, Lake Washington, Lake Union, and all their tributaries.

The Pacific Coast Inlets

Inland Rules apply in Washington State only inside the specific Demarcation Lines drawn across the entrances of the major coastal inlets bordering the Pacific Ocean:

  • Grays Harbor: A line drawn across the seaward extremities of the Entrance Jetties.
  • Willapa Bay: A line drawn from Willapa Bay Light to the westernmost tripod south of Leadbetter Point.
  • Columbia River: A line drawn from the seaward extremity of the North Jetty (155° true) to the South Jetty.
  • Quillayute River: A line drawn from the East Jetty to James Island.

Exam Tip

If a test question asks what rules apply to a vessel operating in Seattle, Tacoma, or Olympia, the answer is always International Rules. You only switch to Inland Rules if you physically transit the coastal inlets to the west.
