The Puget Sound Exception

Despite being entirely enclosed by land, Puget Sound is governed by International Rules (72 COLREGS).

Under 33 CFR § 80.1395, the 72 COLREGS apply to all waters of the Strait of Juan de Fuca, Haro Strait, Strait of Georgia, Puget Sound, Hood Canal, Lake Washington, Lake Union, and Lake Sammamish (as a connected tributary).

The Pacific Coast Inlets

Inland Rules apply in Washington State only inside the specific Demarcation Lines drawn across the entrances of the coastal Pacific inlets:

  • Grays Harbor: A line drawn across the seaward extremities of the Entrance Jetties.
  • Willapa Bay: A line drawn from Willapa Bay Light to the westernmost tripod south of Leadbetter Point.
  • Columbia River: A line drawn from the North Jetty (155° true) to the South Jetty.
  • Quillayute River: A line drawn from the East Jetty to James Island.

Exam Tip

If a test question asks what rules apply to a vessel operating in Seattle, Tacoma, Olympia, Lake Washington, or Lake Sammamish, the answer is always International Rules.
