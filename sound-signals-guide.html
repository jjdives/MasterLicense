Definitions & Equipment

  • Short Blast: A blast of about 1 second duration.
  • Prolonged Blast: A blast of from 4 to 6 seconds duration.
  • Vessels < 12m: Not obliged to carry sound signaling appliances, but must have some other means of making an efficient sound signal.
  • Vessels 12m - 20m: Must carry a whistle.
  • Vessels 20m - 100m: Must carry a whistle and a bell.
  • Vessels > 100m: Must carry a whistle, a bell, and a gong (whose tone cannot be confused with the bell).

Blind Bend / Leaving Dock

  • Nearing a blind bend: Sounded by a vessel nearing a bend or an area where other vessels may be obscured. Answered with 1 prolonged blast by any approaching vessel.
  • Inland Only: Also sounded by a power-driven vessel leaving a dock or berth.

Overtaking Signals

Crucial Difference: International rules require complex signals to denote overtaking and require agreement via a distinct sequence. Inland rules simply use the standard 1 or 2 short blasts as proposals, requiring the exact same signal in return to agree.

Overtaking (International)

  • To pass on Stbd: 2 Prolonged, 1 Short.
  • To pass on Port: 2 Prolonged, 2 Short.
  • Agreement (The "OK" signal): 1 Prolonged, 1 Short, 1 Prolonged, 1 Short.

Overtaking (Inland)

Anywhere vessels are in sight

Click to flip

Overtaking (Inland)

  • To pass on Stbd: 1 Short.
  • To pass on Port: 2 Short.
  • Agreement: Sound the same signal back (1 short or 2 short).
  • Disagreement: Sound the Danger Signal (5+ short blasts).

Restricted Visibility (Fog Signals)

Sounded continuously when in or near an area of restricted visibility. Intervals are "not more than X minutes."

Power-Driven, Underway & Making Way

Click to flip

Making Way

  • Signal: One prolonged blast.
  • Interval: Not more than every 2 minutes.
  • Applies to power-driven vessels actively moving through the water.

Power-Driven, Underway but STOPPED

Click to flip

Stopped (Not Making Way)

  • Signal: Two prolonged blasts.
  • Interval: Not more than every 2 minutes.
  • Applies to power-driven vessels underway but stopped and making no way through the water.

NUC, RAM, CBD, Sailing, Fishing, Towing/Pushing

Click to flip

"The Encumbered Vessels"

  • Signal: One prolonged blast followed by two short blasts.
  • Interval: Not more than every 2 minutes.
  • Used by: Not Under Command, Restricted Ability to Maneuver, Constrained by Draft, Sailing, Fishing, and vessels engaged in Towing or Pushing.

Towed Vessel (Manned)

Click to flip

Towed Vessel

  • Signal: One prolonged blast followed by three short blasts.
  • Interval: Not more than every 2 minutes.
  • Sounded immediately after the towing vessel's signal, if practical. Only required if the towed vessel is manned.

Pilot Vessel (Optional ID)

Pilot Vessel Identity

  • Signal: Four short blasts.
  • May be sounded in addition to their standard required signal (e.g., underway or anchored) to identify themselves as a pilot vessel.

Anchored

  • Signal: Rapid ringing of the bell for 5 seconds.
  • Interval: Not more than every 1 minute.
  • >100m: Bell in forepart, followed by 5 seconds of rapid gong ringing in the aft part.
  • Optional Warning: 1 short, 1 prolonged, 1 short ( • ▬ • ) to warn approaching vessels of position.

Aground

  • Signal: 3 distinct bell strokes + 5 seconds rapid ringing + 3 distinct bell strokes.
  • Interval: Not more than every 1 minute.
  • >100m: Above sequence on the bell, followed by 5 seconds rapid gong ringing aft.
